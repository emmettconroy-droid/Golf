Narin & Portnoo Golf Club

Founded 1905 · Redesigned by Gil Hanse

Ranked among Ireland's finest links courses, Narin & Portnoo sits perched above Gweebarra Bay on the rugged Donegal coast. Gil Hanse's redesign transformed an already spectacular layout into a world-class championship test — without losing an ounce of its wild, authentic character.

For American golfers, this is bucket-list Ireland: rolling fairways carved through Atlantic dunes, greens buffeted by salt winds off the ocean, and views that stretch from Donegal's sea stacks to the islands of Arranmore and Inishkeel.

Why Golfers Choose Donegal

  • Genuine links golf — no two rounds alike
  • Far less crowded than Lahinch or Ballybunion
  • Doorstep access to the Wild Atlantic Way
  • Slieve League — Europe's highest sea cliffs — 30 min away
  • Authentic Irish village experience, no tourist crowds
Irish Weather Tip: Pack waterproof gear regardless of forecast — Donegal weather changes fast. Layer up: thermals, mid-layer, and a quality waterproof jacket. The good news? Wind-swept days produce the most memorable links golf.

Your Home on the Links

Family-run, golfer-focused, and just minutes from the first tee.

Atlantic Links B&B is a family-run guesthouse on Narin Road, a short drive from the first tee at Narin & Portnoo. We've built our hospitality around golfers: early breakfasts before dawn tee times, a warm drying room for wet gear, and a host who knows every caddie, local course, and hidden 19th-hole pub worth visiting.

Our en-suite rooms combine Irish comfort with the practicalities serious golfers need. And you'll never go out hungry — our full Irish breakfast is legendary among returning guests.

Donegal is one of Ireland's last truly unspoiled counties. Beyond the golf, you're on the doorstep of the Wild Atlantic Way, Slieve League's towering sea cliffs, and the Irish-speaking Gaeltacht heartland. We'll help you make the most of every day.
